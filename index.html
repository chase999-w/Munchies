Our Mission

Mystery meat is canceled.

For too long, American school lunches have been an afterthought — frozen, fried, and forgotten. Munchies is changing that. We partner with local farms, chef-driven kitchens, and nutritionists to deliver meals that fuel focus, build strong bodies, and introduce kids to the wide world of food. Because every student deserves a lunch break worth looking forward to.

Fresh Daily

Prepared same-day in regional kitchens. No reheated freezer food — ever.

Nutrient-Dense

Every meal is registered-dietitian approved and meets USDA standards.

Global Flavors

From teriyaki bowls to birria tacos — we open kids' palates to the world.

Affordable

Fits district budgets. Compatible with NSLP and free/reduced programs.
